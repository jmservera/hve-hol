The day at a glance · Suggested agenda

Ten exercises, three intermissions, four open labs

On this pageHow the labs runAgents or thinking

This agenda is a suggestion

Your facilitator confirms the timings on the day. Exercises are numbered, not time-boxed: inside each open lab your team paces itself and picks up the next exercise when it is ready.

Time (suggested)BlockPurpose
09:00–09:30Arrival and technical recoveryResolve final access issues; assign teams and roles
09:30–09:50Kick-off and challenge framingConfirm scenarios, success criteria and working agreements
09:50–11:15Open lab 1 · Exercises 1–3Predict the lifecycle, build an evidence map, define MVEs
11:15–11:30BreakAgents may continue running
11:30–13:00Open lab 2 · Exercise 4 · Intermission AIntake to an approved backlog; pick the Exercise 5 item before lunch so research can run
13:00–14:00LunchShort coach huddle and run health check
14:00–15:30Open lab 3 · Exercises 5–7 · Intermissions B–CBounded RPI build; gates while it runs; reuse a correction after the review
15:30–15:45BreakPrepare evidence for cross-review
15:45–17:10Open lab 4 · Exercises 8 and 9Red team and recovery; adoption path (Exercise 10 is backup if time remains)
17:10–17:30Team playback and closeShow evidence, lessons, decisions, owners and next steps

Choose the working mode

Agents or thinking?

ExerciseHow to work
1 · Predict the lifecycleNo agents: whole-room thinking and answer sheet
2 · Evidence mapPeople classify first; one targeted read-only agent call
3 · MVEsSet thresholds first; agents generate research or prototypes
4 · BacklogAgents draft; the product owner answers and approves
5 · RPIFull RPI run; people approve the plan and accept the result
6 · ReuseOne targeted call to capture and test a lesson from Exercise 5
7 · GatesMostly no agents; make a deterministic control actually block
8 · RecoveryMostly no agents; recovery may re-run one phase
9 · AdoptionMostly no agents; agree the pilot and fill the canvas
10 · ComparisonOptional: agents repeat the work with one variable changed

Tips

  • Exercise 7 begins while Exercise 5 runs, after its plan is approved; the Exercise 4 outputs are already enough to start the gate map.
  • By 15:30, bring RPI artefacts, a gate map and one lesson reused to the cross-review.
  • Exercise 10 is a backup: try it before close only if time remains. Its reference section comes after playback in this guide, but playback stays at 17:10–17:30.
  • If time is short, drop the backup comparison first, then intermissions; protect the core tasks.
  • Adapt the split to your team size and run speed.
