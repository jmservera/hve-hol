The day at a glance · Suggested agenda

Ten exercises, three intermissions, four open labs

This agenda is a suggestion

Your facilitator confirms the timings on the day. Exercises are numbered, not time-boxed: inside each open lab your team paces itself and picks up the next exercise when it is ready.

Time (suggested)BlockPurpose
09:00–09:30Arrival and technical recoveryResolve final access issues; assign teams and roles
09:30–09:50Kick-off and challenge framingConfirm scenarios, success criteria and working agreements
09:50–11:15Open lab 1 · Exercises 1–3Predict the lifecycle, build an evidence map, define MVEs
11:15–11:30BreakAgents may continue running
11:30–13:00Open lab 2 · Exercises 4–5 · Intermission AIntake to an approved backlog; design a reusable component
13:00–14:00LunchShort coach huddle and run health check
14:00–15:30Open lab 3 · Exercises 6–7 · Intermissions B–CBounded RPI build, gates and controls; AI versus script audit
15:30–15:45BreakPrepare evidence for cross-review
15:45–17:10Open lab 4 · Exercises 8 and 10Red team and recovery; adoption path (Exercise 9 is backup)
17:10–17:30Team playback and closeShow evidence, lessons, decisions, owners and next steps

How the labs run · Suggested split

A core task and a parallel task in every lab

Open labCore taskParallel task, while agents runHand in by the end of the lab
Open lab 1Ex 1 · Predict the lifecycle, then Ex 2 · Evidence map
Whole team, then product owner, auditor, agent operator		Ex 3 · MVE bake-off
Architect, quality lead; agents run the experiments (may continue over the break)		Lifecycle map · evidence map · context manifest · questions · 2 MVE cards
Open lab 2Ex 4 · Intake to approved backlog
Product owner, agent operator, quality lead		Ex 5 · Reusable component
Architect and one volunteer; Intermission A (PO canvas) while Ex 4 agents run		Approved requirements · decision log · backlog · component spec · PO canvas
Open lab 3Ex 6 · Bounded RPI work unit
Agent operator; product owner and architect approve the plan		Ex 7 · Gates and guardrails
Quality and governance lead, architect; Intermissions B and C while Ex 6 runs		Research, plan, patch, review · gate map · control matrix · script-versus-AI audit
Open lab 4Ex 8 · Red team and recovery, then Ex 10 · Adoption path
Whole team; paired with another team for Ex 8		Backup · Ex 9 · Compare autonomy, cost and outcome
Auditor / observer, only if there is time; then playback preparation		Findings · recovery log · adoption canvas · pilot and owners

Teams pace themselves inside each lab. The auditor / observer keeps the log all day; it feeds Exercise 8 and the playback.

Tips

  • Exercise 7 is a core task but can start while Exercise 6 executes, because its input can be the Exercise 4 outputs.
  • Exercise 9 is a backup: run it only if your team finishes early.
  • Adapt the split to your team size and run speed.
