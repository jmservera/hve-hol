Exercise 1 · Open lab 1 · Parallel or waiting-time task

Predict the lifecycle before the agents do

Parallel taskOpen lab 1

On this pageBriefHand-in and toolkitRevealTips
Objective

Understand the lifecycle and expose hidden assumptions before automation starts.

Input
A ready-made end-to-end scenario or one of your own cases
Best use
Parallel or waiting-time task
Roles
Whole team; the auditor records the differences

Steps

  1. Look only at the starting evidence and the desired outcome.

  2. On paper, write the steps, roles, gates and artefacts you expect.

  3. The facilitator reveals a pre-run walkthrough or logs, one stage at a time.

  4. At each stage, compare your decision with the actual HVE or HVE-Squad path.

  5. Label each difference: missing context · different judgement · unnecessary machinery.

Hand in

Tick items as your team hands them in.

Exercise 1 · Reveal

The governed path: people own the transitions

  1. Discover

    conversation, ticket or incident → evidence map

  2. Define

    requirements → architecture → backlog

  3. Build

    Research → Plan → Implement → Review

  4. Deliver and operate

    PR review, release and ITSM gates, feedback into the next loop

Five gates, one owner each

  1. Evidence → BRDProduct owner
  2. BRD → PRDProduct + engineering
  3. PRD → PlanArchitect
  4. Plan → ImplementEngineering lead
  5. Review → MergeRelease approver

The recorded run (inventory-aware cart)

  1. 28-min transcript + 3 pages
  2. 7 unresolved questions
  3. 10 functional · 9 non-functional requirements · 15 acceptance criteria
  4. 19 answers from the owner
  5. research
  6. plan + critique: Pass
  7. 20 files · 56 tests
  8. review
  9. owner validation

One recorded demonstration on a sample app, not a benchmark or a productivity claim.

Link

  • HVE Core · AI-assisted project lifecycle (opens in a new tab)

Security and Responsible AI checks sit on these gates, not in a separate review.

Talk about it

  • Who owns each of these gates in your projects today?
  • What was your most surprising difference, and which label did you give it?
  • The same lifecycle covers application management: the trigger is an incident or a service request instead of a new product idea.

Tips

  • If your own case has pre-run material (research, plan and review files of a previous run), use it; otherwise use the inventory-aware cart.
  • The starting evidence is typically a stakeholder transcript and some context; nothing else until the reveal.
  • Keep your map: it is reused in Exercise 9 (backup) to compare predicted and actual human effort.
