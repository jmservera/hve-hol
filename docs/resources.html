Accelerators: start from a proven architecture

Accelerators are ready-made, maintained solution architectures with deployment guides, costing and sample data. Use them as the reference architecture when you research and plan, instead of starting from a blank repo. The featured list is a selection relevant to this workshop; the full catalogue is on accelerators.ms.

How accelerators work

  1. 1 · Browse

    Discover ready-made solutions for your customer need

  2. 2 · Select

    Pick the best fit and adapt it with built-in configuration

  3. 3 · Review

    Check prerequisites and costing against your organisation

  4. 4 · Deploy

    Follow the deployment guide to launch quickly and securely

  5. Included

    Proven architecture, documentation, costing, sample data

Featured accelerators

  • Microsoft AI Solution Acceleratorsaccelerators.msFull catalogue (opens in a new tab)

  • Modernize your Code

    Migrate legacy code to modern languages with AI agents

  • Container-Migration

    Automate container platform migration with AI

  • Multi-Agent Custom Automation Engine

    Orchestrate a group of AI agents · Python, TypeScript, Bicep

  • Deploy Your AI Application in Production

    Secure, extensible environment for AI Foundry workloads

In the workshop · Intermission A: while Exercise 4 runs, the product owner looks for a matching accelerator, cites it as the reference architecture and records the ADR decision: adopt, adapt or build.
