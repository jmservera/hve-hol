Exercise 7 · Open lab 3 · Core task, starts while Exercise 6 runs

Human gates and deterministic guardrails

Core taskOpen lab 3

On this pageBriefHand-in and toolkitTips
Objective

Separate advice from enforcement and decide where people remain accountable.

Input
Outputs from Exercise 4 or 6
Best use
Core task
Roles (suggested)
Quality and governance lead, architect

Steps

  1. List every consequential transition: requirements approval, architecture choice, implementation, merge, release.

  2. Assign an owner and the evidence required for each gate.

  3. Classify each rule: advisory, human gate or deterministic control.

  4. Implement or describe one deterministic control: schema validation, CI check, permission boundary or test.

  5. Simulate a blocked gate and decide what evidence is needed to proceed.

Hand in

Tick items as your team hands them in.

Other deterministic options

  • protected branches with required approvals
  • required pipeline checks
  • CODEOWNERS on docs and ADRs
  • schema / front-matter lint for artefacts
  • read-only tools for research and review
  • agent hooks that block writes
  • an APM policy for what agent packages may be installed

Done when

One control actually blocks something, and the override path names who may override and what evidence they need.

Tips

  • Ask: “What happens today if someone skips this gate?” If the answer is “nothing”, the rule is advisory, whatever the process document says.
  • Agents will sometimes skip a step; design for that.
  • A gitignore entry is a convenience, not a control; the required check is the control. Mark the check as required in the merge-request settings.
  • If your repositories are on GitLab or Bitbucket on-premises, prefer those examples. GitLab merge request approval rules need Premium or Ultimate; protected branches and “pipelines must succeed” work on all tiers.
  • The APM policy engine is an early preview.
  • Reuse the operations loop from Intermission B and the step verifier from Intermission C as inputs to your control matrix.
