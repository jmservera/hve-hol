Common scorecard

One scorecard for every team and every approach

Template cells are editable: type in them, then use Copy as Markdown to paste the result into your repository or wiki. What you type stays in this browser only; nothing is uploaded.

Common scorecard

CriterionWhat to observeRating 1–5
Accepted outcomeDid the result satisfy the agreed scope and acceptance criteria?
Lead timeTime to first useful result and to accepted outcome
Human effortQuestions, corrections, approvals and unplanned interventions
QualityRequirement coverage, technical coherence, tests and operational readiness
TraceabilityCan the team connect evidence → decision → requirement → plan → result?
ControlCould the team stop, redirect, recover and resume safely?
ConsumptionAI and resource consumption relative to the value of the accepted result
ReuseWhat can now be reused as a skill, instruction, control, component or practice?
LearningWhat did the team understand that it did not understand before the run?

Tips

  • Used in the playback and, if it is run, in Exercise 10 (backup). Score from logs and artefacts, not impressions.
