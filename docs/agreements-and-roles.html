How we work today

Five working agreements

  1. 1

    Real, small, bounded

    Use a real change where possible. If the source contains sensitive information, use an anonymised or simplified version.

  2. 2

    Nobody waits for an agent

    Every waiting period has a parallel team activity with a concrete output.

  3. 3

    Method first, tools second

    HVE is the operating model, HVE Core a selectable toolkit, HVE-Squad an orchestration option. Not a contest to crown one tool.

  4. 4

    Enforce what is deterministic

    Deterministic controls for deterministic checks; agents where judgement, synthesis or exploration is needed.

  5. 5

    Measure what matters

    Accepted outcomes, human intervention, traceability and learning; not the volume of generated code or tokens.

Strengthen what you already do · No magic multiplier · Your code stays on your own machines

Team roles

Five roles in every team

  • Product owner / domain lead

    Frames the outcome, answers questions and accepts or rejects requirements.

  • Architect

    Surfaces constraints, risks, dependencies and architecture decisions.

  • Agent operator

    Runs the chosen HVE Core or HVE-Squad flow and keeps the session moving.

  • Quality and governance lead

    Defines acceptance criteria, controls, security and Responsible AI checks, and human gates.

  • Auditor / observer

    Records assumptions, human interventions, timings, failures, cost signals and lessons.

~25 participants · 4–5 multidisciplinary teams · at least one experienced agent-mode user in every team · in small teams, one person can hold two roles

  • Your code stays on your laptops: facilitators never need access to it.
