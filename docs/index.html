Today

This is not another presentation

What today is for

  1. 1

    Experience a governed agentic lifecycle, end to end, on your own scenario

  2. 2

    Compare approaches: HVE Core, HVE-Squad or your own orchestrator

  3. 3

    Leave with a concrete view of where HVE fits into your existing engineering and AMS practices

North-star outcome

Each team finishes with evidence, decisions, a tested increment or a credible implementation plan, and one adoption commitment that continues after Microsoft leaves the room.

The question isn’t which tool wins. It’s where each approach adds the most value.

How to use this guide

  • Follow along at your own pace

    Your facilitator presents and coordinates; this site has every brief, template and link, so your team can keep working without waiting for the screen.

  • Open labs, not time slots

    Exercises are numbered, not time-boxed. Teams work in four open labs and start the next exercise when they are ready.

  • Brief, starting guidance and toolkit

    Read the brief, then How to start for Exercises 2–10: four first steps, practical advice and traps to avoid. The toolkit has fill-in templates, commands and links. Exercise 1 is a guided, no-agent warm-up with an answer sheet and six reveals.

  • Nobody waits for an agent

    Intermissions A–C fill waiting time while agents run. Exercise 10 is a backup for teams that finish early.

Template cells are editable: type in them, then use Copy as Markdown to paste the result into your repository or wiki. What you type stays in this browser only; nothing is uploaded.
