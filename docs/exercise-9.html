Exercise 9 · Open lab 4 · Core task

Design your adoption path

Core taskOpen lab 4

On this pageBriefHow to startHand-in and toolkitTips
Objective

Turn workshop learning into a repeatable internal practice and a real pilot.

Input
The day’s artefacts and lessons
Best use
Core task
Roles
Whole team; product owner and architect lead

Steps

  1. Choose where HVE fits in your organisation’s current lifecycle, and where it does not.

  2. Define the minimum shared practice: context, gates, evidence, reuse and metrics.

  3. Select one Customer Zero or AMS / modernisation pilot.

  4. Name a sponsor, practice owner, technical owner and first participating team.

  5. Set 30-day success criteria and a review rhythm.

Hand in

Tick items as your team hands them in.

Exercise 9 · How to start

From a good day to a 30-day pilot

Start here

  1. 1

    Everyone writes two lines: one thing from today we would use on Monday, and one we would not.

  2. 2

    Group them, then fill the adoption canvas from top to bottom.

  3. 3

    Pick one real pilot with an owner in the room: an AMS stream, a modernisation slice or Customer Zero.

  4. 4

    Set two or three measures you can baseline today, and a date for the 30-day review.

Tips and tricks

  • Reuse what you built today: the Exercise 6 skill, the Exercise 7 control, your templates.
  • Prefer measures your organisation already reports: lead time, rework, interventions per item.
  • Names, not departments: sponsor, practice owner, technical owner, first team.
  • Write down what is out of scope and which approvals you still need: security, legal, data.

Watch out

  • “Roll out HVE everywhere”
  • A pilot without a baseline
  • No date for the first review

Done when

The pilot has named owners, a start date and acceptance criteria someone could measure in 30 days.

Tips

  • Aim to industrialise the agentic development practice you already have, measure its impact and start with a controlled Customer Zero group.
  • Use names, not roles, and measure a baseline before the pilot starts.
  • Pick two metrics your organisation already reports rather than inventing new ones (for example lead time and rework from unclear requirements).
  • Typical pilot scorecard: unplanned intervention rate, active human minutes per item, AI cost per accepted item, lead time and quality guardrails.
  • Name the Azure DevOps/GitHub scope and its owners. If using Work IQ, also name the approved SharePoint/Teams sources, access owner and any preview/billing readiness work still needed.
